Staxx Terms of Use

Last updated: October 14, 2026

These are the rules for using Staxx. They are short on purpose. By creating an account you agree to them, along with our Privacy Policy.

You must be 13 or older

Staxx is not for children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not create an account. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will remove it.

What you may not post

The things you can publish on Staxx are your username, your profile picture, and the reviews you write about books you have read. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content in any of them. Specifically, you may not post a username, profile picture or review that is:

  • abusive, harassing, threatening, or intended to bully a particular person;
  • hateful, or that attacks or demeans people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, or age;
  • sexual, pornographic, or sexually suggestive;
  • violent, graphic, or gratuitously disturbing;
  • impersonating another person, or pretending to be Staxx staff;
  • illegal, or promoting illegal activity;
  • spam, advertising, or a scam.

Some usernames are rejected automatically. That filter is a floor, not a ceiling: a name that gets past it is still against these rules if it breaks the list above.

Reviews are not filtered automatically, on purpose. Books deal with violence, sex, bigotry and cruelty, and an honest review often has to name what it is reviewing. A word filter would flag exactly the reviews most worth reading. Reviews are moderated by report instead - which means we rely on you to tell us. Writing about what happens in a book is fine; attacking a person is not.

A review can be up to 15,000 characters. You can edit or delete your own reviews at any time from the book's page, and deleting your ranking of a book deletes the review with it.

Reporting, and what we do about it

Every profile has a ··· menu with a Report option, and every review has a Report link beneath it. Tell us what is wrong and, if you like, add a note.

We review every report within 24 hours. If we agree that content breaks these rules we will act on it, which can mean removing a profile picture, resetting a username, suspending the account, or deleting it. Accounts that repeat the behaviour, or that do something serious once, are removed without warning. We do not tell the person who reported what we decided about someone else's account.

Removing a review does not remove the ranking it came with: the book stays on that person's shelf with its score, and only the written text goes.

Blocking

You can block anyone from the same ··· menu. Blocking is mutual and immediate: neither of you will see the other's profile, rankings, shelf, or activity anywhere in Staxx, and you will not appear in each other's searches or follower lists. Any follow between you is removed. You can see everyone you have blocked, and undo it, in Settings › Blocked users.

You do not have to report someone to block them, and you do not have to explain why.

Your account

  • Keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • One account per person. Do not create a new account to get around a suspension.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Delete account. It is immediate and permanent: your rankings, shelf, comparison history, follows, activity, and profile picture are all deleted.

Your content

Your username, profile picture and reviews remain yours. By posting them you give us permission to show them inside Staxx to other users - which is the whole point of a social app. Note that profile pictures are served from a public address; see the Privacy Policy for what that means.
