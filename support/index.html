What Staxx is

Staxx is a book-ranking app. Instead of giving a book a star rating, you say roughly how you felt about it and then compare it against a few books you have already ranked. Those comparisons place it on a 0-10 scale and in order against everything else you have read. You keep two separate lists, fiction and non-fiction, and a To Be Read list of what is coming next. You can follow friends and see what they are reading and how they ranked it.

How do I write or change a review?

After you rank a book you will be asked for a few vibe tags and then offered a box to write a review. It is optional - tap Skip if you would rather not.

To add, change or delete a review later, open the book and use Edit under "Your review". Reviews can be up to 15,000 characters. Anyone who can see your rankings can see your reviews; removing your ranking of a book removes the review with it.

How do I report someone?

Open their profile, tap the ··· menu at the top right, and choose Report. To report a specific review, tap Report underneath it in the feed. Pick a reason and add a note if you want to. We review every report within 24 hours. You will also be offered the option to block them at the same time.

How do I block someone?

Same ··· menu, then Block. Blocking works both ways straight away: neither of you will see the other's profile, rankings, shelf or activity anywhere in the app, neither of you will turn up in the other's searches or follower lists, and any follow between you is removed.

To see who you have blocked, or to undo it, go to Profile › Settings › Blocked users and tap Unblock. Unblocking does not restore a follow - if you want to follow each other again, do it deliberately.

How do I change my profile picture?

Go to the Profile tab and tap your picture, then choose a photo. It is resized on your phone before it is uploaded. Profile pictures are served from a public address, so please do not use anything you would not want seen outside the app.

How do I switch between light and dark?

Profile › Settings › Appearance, then System, Light or Dark. System follows your phone's own setting. Colours also shift between fiction and non-fiction, which is intentional.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile › Settings › Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap the button.

This is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes your account, your rankings and their history, your shelf and To Be Read list, every comparison you have made, your follows and followers, your activity, your tag suggestions, your profile and your profile picture file. Books you added to the shared catalogue stay, because other people use them, but they are no longer linked to you.

I ranked the wrong book

Open the book and choose Remove from my rankings, or swipe left on it in your ranked shelf. The rest of your list re-scores itself automatically. You can also Re-rank a book at any time to move it.

A book's details are wrong

Book titles, authors, covers and descriptions come from Open Library. If something is wrong, email us and we will correct it in our copy.
